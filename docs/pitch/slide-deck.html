The Full Vision

From booking tool to complete clinic platform.

Phase 1 — Grow

Scale to every clinic

Let any clinic self-onboard and run without us.
  • Self-serve onboarding Clinics sign up, connect WhatsApp, and go live without any manual setup.
  • Multi-clinic & chains One dashboard across locations, with full data isolation per clinic.
  • Staff invites & roles Admins add doctors, nurses, receptionists and set permissions in-portal.
  • Service-based booking Patients book by need ("blood pressure check") — matched to the right doctor.
Phase 2 — Care

Beyond the appointment

Keep patients healthy between visits.
  • Medication adherence Gentle WhatsApp nudges — "Have you taken your medicine today?"
  • Lab test follow-ups Reminders to take tests and alerts when results are ready to collect.
  • Next-visit reminders Proactive nudges for recurring check-ups and chronic care.
  • Patient history & records A richer patient view with visit history and nurse workflows.
Phase 3 — Earn

A full business platform

Revenue and clinical workflows, done right.
  • Payments over WhatsApp Collect consultation fees via Razorpay / Stripe — never touch card data.
  • Invoices & receipts Automatic digital receipts sent straight to the patient's WhatsApp.
  • E-prescriptions Compliant digital prescriptions where law permits, with doctor sign-off.
  • Waitlist & smart fill Auto-offer freed slots to waiting patients when a cancellation happens.
Each phase builds on the last. Clinical and payment features ship only where compliant — patient privacy and local regulations come first.
