# DoctorDesk — Timed Meeting Script (Doctor)

> A full 20-minute meeting, start to finish, with timings. Word-for-word lines you can
> say, plus _italic stage directions_. **Bold = the line that must land.**
>
> This is a conversation, not a monologue. The [ASK] blocks are where you stop and
> listen — a doctor who talks is a doctor who buys. Adjust on the fly; the timings
> are a guide, not a cage.
>
> **Total: ~20 min** (12 min you, ~8 min them). If you only have 10 min, use the
> "Short version" at the bottom. Shared messaging (objections, elevator, vision,
> honesty guardrails) lives in `_shared-messaging.md`.

---

## 0:00–1:30 — Open & set the frame (90 sec)

_Warm, relaxed. Phone with WhatsApp demo ready. Portal open in a browser tab._

> "Thank you for making time, doctor. I know you're busy, so I'll keep this to about
> fifteen minutes and leave plenty of room for your questions.
>
> I've built something for clinics, and before I show it, I'd love to understand how
> your clinic runs today — so I'm showing you what's actually useful, not just a
> generic demo. Is that okay?"

_Wait for the yes. This earns permission and makes it a conversation._

---

## 1:30–4:00 — Discovery: understand their world (2.5 min)

_This is the most important part. Ask, then shut up and listen. Take mental notes —
you'll use their answers in the pitch._

> **"How do your patients book an appointment today?"**

_Let them answer fully. Then follow up with 2-3 of these, whatever fits:_

- "Roughly how many patients do you see in a day? And how many doctors?"
- "Who handles the phone and the scheduling — one receptionist, a few?"
- "How often do patients just not show up? What does that cost you in a week?"
- "Do you use any software for this today, or is it mostly phone and a register?"
- "Does the clinic already have a WhatsApp number patients message you on?"

> **"If you could wave a wand and fix one thing about scheduling tomorrow, what would
> it be?"**

_Remember this answer. You'll echo it back during the demo and the close._

---

## 4:00–5:00 — Bridge to the problem (60 sec)

_Reflect their own words back. Make them feel understood._

> "So if I heard you right — [repeat their biggest pain: missed calls / no-shows /
> front desk overloaded]. That's exactly the problem I set out to solve.
>
> Here's the thing: your patients are already on WhatsApp all day — messaging family,
> paying bills, ordering food. **But to book you, they still have to call during
> office hours and hope someone picks up.** That gap is where you lose patients and
> where your front desk drowns."

---

## 5:00–8:00 — The live demo (3 min) ⭐ THE HEART

_Do this LIVE on your phone if at all possible. The slide is a backup. Slow down._

> "Let me just show you. I'm a patient. I text the clinic's WhatsApp number..."

_Type / tap it out:_

> "'I need an appointment.' — and instantly it asks me to pick a doctor."
>
> _Tap 1._ "I pick one. Now it shows me only the days that actually have openings."
>
> _Tap a day._ "I pick a day — and only the free times for that day."
>
> _Tap a time._ "I pick a time... and **done. Confirmed.** That was under a minute,
> and it works at 11pm on a Sunday just as well as Monday morning."

_Pause. Let it sink in. Then the credibility line:_

> **"And here's the part I'm proudest of — it is impossible to double-book.** If two
> patients tap the same slot at the same second, only one gets it and the other is
> instantly offered the next opening. That's not a setting someone can forget to turn
> on — the system literally cannot accept two bookings for the same slot."

[ASK] _"Have you ever been double-booked by mistake?"_ — _they almost always have.
Nod and move on._

---

## 8:00–10:00 — The staff side (2 min)

_Switch to the portal tab._

> "Now, while that patient was booking, your team sees it here — in real time, no app
> to install, no training.
>
> Today's appointments, the whole week, every patient at a glance. One click to
> confirm someone, mark them complete, or flag a no-show.
>
> And setting a doctor's hours — a day off, a lunch break, extra evening hours — takes
> seconds, and the WhatsApp booking updates automatically. Your staff never touches a
> line of anything technical.
>
> **If you cancel or move an appointment here, the patient gets a WhatsApp message
> instantly — no one has to call them.**"

---

## 10:00–11:30 — Reminders & self-service (90 sec)

> "Two more things that directly hit no-shows:
>
> One — **automatic reminders on WhatsApp.** A WhatsApp message gets read about 98% of
> the time. A reminder phone call? Maybe 20%. That difference is money back in your
> pocket.
>
> Two — patients can **cancel or reschedule themselves**, right in the chat. That slot
> frees up automatically for someone else, and your front desk never picks up the
> phone for it."

[ASK] _"How are you reminding patients today?"_ — _lets them feel the contrast._

---

## 11:30–13:30 — The vision: where it's going (2 min)

_Energy up. You're not selling a tool, you're selling a direction._

> "Everything I've shown you works today. But this is just the foundation. There are
> three phases ahead:
>
> **Grow** — any clinic signs itself up, multiple locations, patients booking by what
> they need, like 'a blood pressure check.'
>
> **Care** — keeping patients healthy between visits: medication reminders, lab-test
> follow-ups, nudges for recurring check-ups.
>
> **Earn** — collecting consultation fees right over WhatsApp, digital receipts, even
> e-prescriptions where the law allows.
>
> And I want to be straight with you — **the clinical and payment features only roll
> out where they're legal and the patient has consented. We never send lab results
> over WhatsApp.** Your patients' privacy comes first, always."

[ASK] **"Of everything I've described — today's features and what's coming — what would
help your clinic the most?"**

_This is gold. Their answer tells you exactly what to emphasize and signals how bought-in
they are. Listen hard._

---

## 13:30–16:30 — Open conversation / objections (3 min)

_Hand them space. Answer whatever comes. The full objection-handling table (older
patients, "we already have a system," privacy, WhatsApp downtime, cost, setup
effort) is in `_shared-messaging.md` — keep it glanceable. Note the honesty
guardrail: say "building toward HIPAA/DPDP compliance," not "fully compliant."_

---

## 16:30–19:00 — The close (2.5 min)

_Confident, specific, low-pressure. You're offering value, not asking a favor._

> "So here's what I'd love to do.
>
> **I'll set DoctorDesk up for your clinic as a free pilot.** No cost, no commitment —
> I just want your honest feedback to make it better. I handle all the setup; you and
> your team just start seeing bookings come in on WhatsApp.
>
> We start small, you watch it work with your own patients, and if it's not making
> your days easier, we walk away — no hard feelings."

_Then the specific ask — don't soften it:_

> **"Can we book 30 minutes this week to get your clinic set up?"**

_STOP TALKING. Let them answer. Silence is your friend here._

_If yes:_ pin a day and time right now. "Perfect — does Thursday afternoon work?"

_If hesitant:_ "No pressure at all. What would you need to see to feel comfortable
trying it?" — _then address exactly that._

_If no / not now:_ "Completely understand. Can I check back with you in two weeks once
you've had time to think?" — _get a specific date._

---

## 19:00–20:00 — Wrap (60 sec)

> "Thank you, doctor — this was really helpful, genuinely. Here's a one-page summary
> to keep." _(Hand over the printed one-pager.)_
>
> "I'll follow up [the specific day you agreed]. And if any questions come up before
> then, just message me — I'm on WhatsApp too." _(smile)_

_Leave on a warm, human note. The relationship matters more than this one meeting._

---

## Short version (10 minutes)

If time is tight, collapse to this:

| Time | Section |
|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | Open + one discovery question: "How do patients book today?" |
| 1:00–4:00 | Live demo: 60-sec booking + zero double-booking |
| 4:00–5:30 | Staff portal + reminders (quick) |
| 5:30–6:30 | Vision in one breath: Grow / Care / Earn + privacy line |
| 6:30–7:00 | The one question: "What would help you most?" |
| 7:00–9:00 | Objections / their questions |
| 9:00–10:00 | Close: free pilot + book the setup call |

---

## Pre-meeting checklist

- [ ] Phone charged, WhatsApp open to the demo clinic, test one booking beforehand
- [ ] Portal open in a browser tab, logged in, showing sample appointments
- [ ] Printed one-pager to leave behind
- [ ] Deck as backup (in case the live demo fails — never rely only on live)
- [ ] A specific day/time in mind to propose for the setup call
- [ ] Know your honest answers: what's live vs. coming, and the rough pricing direction

## The 3 things to land no matter what

1. **Under 60 seconds to book** (the demo)
2. **Impossible to double-book** (the guarantee)
3. **Free pilot, I do the setup** (the close)

Everything else is supporting cast.
